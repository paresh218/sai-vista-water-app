How to interpret this report
Consumption: wing-level “Water Used” readings from the uploaded sensor reports.
PCMC received: “Water Received” from the PCMC inlet tank report.
September: month-to-date data, not a complete month. Compare average/day rather than raw monthly totals.
Leakage caution: differences among PCMC, main tanks and wing readings are not automatically leakage. Tank-level sensors, timing, tank geometry and simultaneous inflow/outflow can create reconciliation differences.

Transparency & source methodology

Wing coverage
A, B, C, D, E and F wings are included separately for Domestic, Drinking and Flushing water.
Periods
August 2026 and September 2026 month-to-date are available. Coverage days come directly from the consolidated daily exports.
Data source
This static dashboard embeds the consolidated values extracted from the supplied tank and wing Excel sensor exports. No server-side database is required.
